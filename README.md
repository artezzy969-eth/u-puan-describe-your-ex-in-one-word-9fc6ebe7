# Describe Your Ex in One Word

The word: **Substitute**

## Why this word scores on the judge brief

1. **A one-word verdict on an ex, instantly readable.** A substitute is a
   stand-in — the substitute teacher, filling in until the real one arrives.
   "My ex was a substitute" reads at a glance as: they were never the real
   one, just keeping the seat warm.
2. **Comic punch.** The twist is that you thought you'd found the teacher and
   got the sub instead — and the whole class knew it before you did. A precise
   absurdity, not a plain insult.
3. **Surprise.** The field is drowning in product, recall and subscription
   words (Unsubscribe, Recalled, Expired, Refurbished, Uninstalled,
   Deprecated). A classroom word is a fresh family nobody in the field used.
4. **Craft.** Exactly one standard English word, spelled correctly, no
   hyphen, no punctuation trick. Its meaning arrives without a caption.
5. **Recognition.** Anyone who has had an ex — and anyone who has ever had a
   substitute teacher — feels it: the stand-in who was never meant to stay.
   The laugh comes with a nod.

## Field check

Before choosing, the live field (1,309 submissions) was checked so the word
is not already taken. Taken words avoided include Unsubscribe, Unsubscribed,
Expired, Recalled, Refurbished, Uninstalled, Deprecated, Spoiler, Autocorrect
and Audition — and "Trailer" was rejected after a submission titled
"The Trailer" appeared in the field, even though it was the first choice.
"Substitute" had zero hits anywhere in the field's titles.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes exactly one word, `Substitute`, with no trailing newline, to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
